## 💫 About Me

I am a final-year **Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)** undergraduate at **American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB)** (2022–2026). I combine full-stack engineering with applied machine learning research to build high-performance web systems and computer vision solutions.

- 🎓 **Education:** B.Sc. in CSE at American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB).
- 🔬 **Research:** Part-time IT & Machine Learning Researcher at **NEDAAS Lab** (Neural Engineering, Data Analytics & Applied Science). Co-authored **5 peer-reviewed research papers** presented at IEEE conferences, including ICCIT, SPICSCON, and QPAIN.
- 💻 **Full-Stack Engineering:** Proficient in TypeScript, React.js, Next.js, Node.js, Express.js, FastAPI, MongoDB, PostgreSQL, and Firebase.
- 🚩 **Competitive Security:** Active CTF player with **Team ChatGPT** — 4th place in AIUB Inter-University CTF 2026 Prelims and Top-30 Grand Finalist.
- 🎮 **Passions:** Software architecture, deep learning research, CTF challenges, and story-driven gaming.
